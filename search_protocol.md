# Coverage-oriented search protocol

This artifact documents the protocol described in the manuscript. It is a structured mapping protocol, not a claim of exhaustive bibliometric coverage.

## Seed sources
The search is seeded by prior adversarial-XAI/security systematizations and intrinsic-model surveys cited in the manuscript.

## Expansion
1. Backward and forward snowballing from seed and architecture papers.
2. Targeted combinations of carrier terms (concept bottleneck, prototype, self-explaining, neural additive, explanation bottleneck) with trustworthiness terms (adversarial, backdoor, robustness, privacy, intervention, verification, misalignment).

## Inclusion
Include a work when it directly evaluates a security/reliability/verification property of an intrinsic carrier, establishes carrier semantics needed to interpret a security claim, or provides an explicitly marked boundary mechanism needed to formulate a transfer question.

## Exclusion from quantitative coverage
Exclude works that only mention interpretability/security without relevant evidence, duplicates, and material used only for broad background.

## Coding
Record carrier family, evidence role, attack/failure locus, and primary ICS property informed. Direct and adjacent/boundary evidence must not be conflated.
